IGI Insurance

IGI Auto Cover is an amazing auto insurance plan for individuals and corporate covering automobiles against any damage and risk.

Comprehensive Insurance Plan

Loss and Damage Coverage

In case of any accident, fire and external explosion, theft, riots and strikes, terrorism, natural calamities or total loss the insurance plan covers it.

Liability to Third Party

Damage to the auto (property) or any bodily injury to the third party is taken care of (except for the property held in trust or custody by the insurer of a third party).
