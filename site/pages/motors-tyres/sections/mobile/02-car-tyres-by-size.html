How To Read Tyre Size

Width

205 is the width of the tyre which is measured from one sidewall to other sidewall in millimeters.

Aspect Ratio

55 denotes the aspect ratio which is the distance between the wheel and edge of tyre, looking it from the side. It is also known as profile of tyre.

Rim Size

16 is the rim size or the diameter of the inside of the tyre in inches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you judge tyre conditions?
Evaluate the tread depth of your tyres. Look at the tread and sidewalls for any cuts, scrapes, punctures, bulges, bumps or cracks. Check Tyre Pressure.
How do you read tyre dates?
The chronological age of any tyre can be found on the tyre sidewall by examining the characters following the symbol "DOT". The last four numbers identify the date of manufacture of the tyre to the nearest week. The first two of these four numbers identify the week of manufacture (which range from "01" to "53")
What do numbers mean on tyre size?
Let’s take an example of tyre size 205/55 R16 91V. 205 is the width of the tyre tread in millimeters. 55 refers to the profile of the tyre, or the distance between the wheel and the edge of the tyre, looking at it from the side. The letter R means “radial” which is the way the tyre is constructed. 16 is the rim size, or the diameter of the inside of the tyre. V is for speed rating.
Which brand is the best tyre for a car?
There are multiple popular brands which produce high quality tyres. These include, but not limited to, Michelin, Yokohama, Dunlop, Bridgestone, Continental, Goodyear, MRF etc.
What are the 3 types of tyres?
Three main types of car tyres are All-season, summer and winter. Out of these, all-season is the most popular car tyre type.
