How To Read Tyre Size

Width

205 is the width of the tyre which is measured from one sidewall to other sidewall in millimeters.

Aspect Ratio

55 denotes the aspect ratio which is the distance between the wheel and edge of tyre, looking it from the side. It is also known as profile of tyre.

Rim Size

16 is the rim size or the diameter of the inside of the tyre in inches.
