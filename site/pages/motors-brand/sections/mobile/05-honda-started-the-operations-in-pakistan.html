Honda started the operations in Pakistan by partnering with Atlas Group in 1992. Since then, it has strengthened its roots in the country by introducing popular cars such as City and Civic. The company introduced 5th gen Civic and 3rd gen City in 1992 (CBU) and 1997 respectively. After these sedans, the Honda Atlas cars also introduced the Accord (premium sedan) in the country. It also introduced the Honda CRV (compact crossover SUV). In 2017, the company brought a 7-seater sub-compact SUV, BRV.
