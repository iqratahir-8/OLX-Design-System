Honda Overview

Honda is among the world’s largest and most popular automobile manufacturers. This Japanese company has been the leader when it comes to motorcycles since 1959. Its cars are also popular all over the world.

Honda started the operations in Pakistan by partnering with Atlas Group in 1992. Since then, it has strengthened its roots in the country by introducing popular cars such as City and Civic. The company introduced 5th gen Civic and 3rd gen City in 1992 (CBU) and 1997 respectively. After these sedans, the Honda Atlas cars also introduced the Accord (premium sedan) in the country. It also introduced the Honda CRV (compact crossover SUV). In 2017, the company brought a 7-seater sub-compact SUV, BRV.

Honda cars price start from Rs.3,264,000 and goes up to Rs.15,499,000.

Here is the list of Honda cars in Pakistan

Honda Civic

The 5th generation Honda Civic was introduced in Pakistan in 1992. At that time, it was sold as CBU until its production started in 1994. The latest 11th generation Honda Civic is now available in Pakistan. It comes in three variants (1.5L Turbo M-CVT, Oriel 1.5L Turbo M-CVT, and RS 1.5L Turbo LL-CVT). There are some differences in the engine specs and features of these variants.

Features

Their common features include, electric parking brake, auto brake hold, ABS, vehicle stability assist, hill start assist, emergency stop signal, driver attention monitor, auto door lock, security alarm, etc.

Generations

The 1st generation of Honda Civic (hatchback) was introduced in 1972 and was available until 1979.

Its 2nd generation was introduced in 1979 and its production ended in 1983.

The 3rd generation Civic was introduced in 1983 and was produced until 1987.

Honda Civic 4th generation came in 1987 and remained available until 1991.

Its 5th generation came in 1991 and its production ended in 1995.

The 6th generation Civic came in 1995 and was available until 2000.

The Civic 7th generation was introduced in 2000 and remained available until 2005.

Its 8th generation came in 2005 and its production ended in 2011.

Honda Civic 9th generation was introduced in 2011 and remained available until 2015.

The 10th generation Civic came in 2015 and remained available until 2021.

Its 11th and the latest generation was introduced in 2021 and is still being produced.

Honda City

The 3rd generation Honda City was introduced in 1997 in Pakistan. Currently, its 7th generation is available globally but not in Pakistan. The 6th generation City is being sold in Pakistan at the moment. It is offered in 5 variants (1.2L MT, 1.2L CVT, 1.5L CVT, 1.5 L Aspire MT, and 1.5 L Aspire CVT). Their engine specs and features vary depending upon the variant.

Generations

City 1st generation (hatchback) was introduced in 1981 and remained until 1986.

The 2nd generation City (hatchback) came into existence in 1986 and remained in production until 1993.

Its 3rd generation was introduced after some delay in 1996 and remained in production until 2002.

The 4th generation City came in 2002 and its production ended in 2008.

Its 5th generation was introduced in 2008 and remained available until 2014.

Honda City 6th generation came in 2014 and is still being produced in some countries.

Its 7th generation was introduced in 2019 and is still being produced.

Honda Accord is a premium sedan offered by the company in Pakistan. Currently, its latest generation (10th) is available in Pakistan. Its 1.5 L DOHC inline 4 Cylinders, 16 Valves , VTEC Turbo PGM -FI, 1,498cc engine produces around 180 hp and 233 N.m torque.

Features

Cruise control, active noise control, auto wiper, remote engine start, dual-zone climate control, wireless charger, ABS, EBD, brake assist, hill start assist, lane watch, vehicle stability assist, 6 airbags, etc.

Generations

The 1st generation Accord was introduced in 1976 and remained in production until 1982.

Its 2nd generation was introduced in 1981 and its production ended in 1985.

The Accord 3rd generation came in 1985 and remained in production until 1989.

Its 4th generation was introduced in 1989 and its production ended in 1993.

The 5th generation of Accord came in 1993 and remained in production until 1997.

Its 6th generation came in 1998 and remained in the market until 2002.

The 7th generation Accord was introduced in 2002 and its production ended in 2009.

Its 8th generation came in 2007 and remained in production until 2012.

The Accord 9th generation was introduced in 2012 and remained in production until 2017.

The 10th generation (latest) Accord came in 2018 and is still being produced till now.

Honda CR-V

5th generation Honda CR-V is a compact crossover SUV available in Pakistan. Its i-VTEC, SOHC, 16-valve, In-line 4 cylinder, 1,997cc engine produces around 113 hp and 189 N.m torque.

Features Dual-zone climate control, cruise control, paddle shifters, ABS, EBD, brake assist, hill start assist, automatic brake hold, airbags, security alarm, auto wipers, etc.

Honda BR-V

Atlas Honda introduced the 7-seater sub-compact SUV BRV (1st generation) in Pakistan in 2017. Its 2nd generation is already available globally though. Its 4 Cylinder, 16 Valve, SOHC i-VTEC, electronic fuel injection, 1,497cc engine produces around 118 hp and 145 N.m torque.

Features 16-inch alloy wheels, DRLs, 7-inch display, 4 speakers, airbags, rear camera, ABS, EBD, security alarm, etc.

In future, Honda is expected to locally manufacture Vezel/HR-V in Pakistan.
