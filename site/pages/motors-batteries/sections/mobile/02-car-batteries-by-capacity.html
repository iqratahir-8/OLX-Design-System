Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a car battery cost in Pakistan?
Car Batteries prices range from PKR 3,000 to PKR 7000, depending on the battery size and type.
Which battery is best for cars dry or liquid?
Dry cell batteries tend to be less expensive and less likely to last for an extended lifespan, but gel cell batteries are easier to keep stable at all temperatures, making them more consistently efficient across different driving circumstances.
How long do car batteries usually last?
Some cars will get up to five or six years out of their battery, while others will need a new one after only two years. In general, your car will usually need a new battery after three to four years.
How can you tell if car battery needs replacing?
There can be multiple things to look at.
  • The Engine Is Slow to Turn Over
  • The Dashboard Lights Flicker
  • The Headlights Go Dim When Idling
  • Corrosion is Present on the battery
What kills the battery in a car?
  • You Left Your Headlights On
  • Your Battery Connections Are Loose Or Corroded
  • It's Extremely Hot Or Cold Outside.
  • The Battery Isn't Charging While You Drive
  • Your Battery Is Old.
